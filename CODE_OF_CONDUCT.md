# Community Conduct

We welcome participants with different backgrounds and experience levels who share the goal of making technology safer.

Collaborate respectfully. Critique technical claims, not people. Do not harass, intimidate, discriminate against, or expose private information about others. Do not pressure contributors to conduct tests outside authorized scope.

Maintainers may moderate discussions or reject material that undermines security, privacy, or constructive collaboration. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through an appropriate private channel; avoid including sensitive personal information in public issues.

This is an original, concise community policy and not a claim of adopting an external code-of-conduct version.
